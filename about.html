Biography

Andreas Krings is a surrealist painter working in Berlin. His canvases take the ordinary — a chair, a suitcase, an hourglass — and set it adrift in a world where the rules of light, weight, and time no longer apply.

Each painting begins as a small ink drawing. The idea is tested on paper first — the composition, the impossibility — and only then is it translated to canvas, where it grows slowly, over months, in thin layers of oil.

He has exhibited across Asia and Europe since 2012. His work sits in collections in Berlin, Osaka, London, and New York.
